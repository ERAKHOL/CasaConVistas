Welcome to our place

Relax in the heart of Andalusia

Welcome to our home and accommodation in the beautiful Andalusian countryside, located in Coín, Málaga.

Set in a peaceful and natural environment, our property offers the perfect combination of tranquillity, comfort and easy access to the many attractions of southern Spain.

Málaga Airport is approximately 30 minutes away, while Marbella and Costa del Sol can be reached in around 25–30 minutes.

Explore the property

A place to slow down

The property is a peaceful green oasis nestled on the hillside above Coín, surrounded by nature and breathtaking views across the Andalusian countryside. Here, life moves at a slower pace. The atmosphere is quiet and private, with greenery all around and wide-open views stretching across the landscape. As we continue developing the property, we are planting more trees, growing our own vegetables and creating a more sustainable and organically minded way of living. This gallery offers a glimpse of the place, the surrounding nature and the small details that make our corner of Andalusia so special.

Growing with nature

From our land to your table

We are gradually developing our land and creating a place where accommodation, nature and sustainable living come together.

We grow our own vegetables and have several fruit trees on the property. Our ambition is to offer organically grown vegetables and fruit for sale from spring 2027.

Discover the region

Authentic Andalusia, close to everything

Coín offers traditional Spanish atmosphere, local restaurants, cafés, shops and markets. The surrounding countryside is ideal for walking and hiking, while the Costa del Sol, beaches and several excellent golf courses are within easy reach.

Whether you are looking for relaxation, outdoor activities, great food or a base for exploring southern Spain, our place offers a wonderful combination of comfort, tranquillity and authentic Spanish charm.
